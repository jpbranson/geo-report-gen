This profile describes {area} with public data from federal statistical agencies. It compares
the area with {benchmark_list} where a comparison is meaningful and shows how conditions changed
over time where comparable data exist.

Survey values are 5-year period estimates with 90 percent margins of error. A survey value is
called higher or lower than another only when the difference is statistically significant; when
no margin of error is available, the report says the difference is untested. Values that could
not be shown, and why, are listed at the end of the report.
